# Zumda brand kit

The mark is a map pin with a house: "your order, to your home" (owner's choice, October 2026,
scheme "Bog'"). The word is "zumda", lowercase, in outlines (Rubik Bold, SIL Open Font
License): the files need no font installed.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Bog' (green) | `#15803D` | The pin, buttons where no shop leads, the default shop color |
| Archa (deep green) | `#14532D` | The word "zumda" on light backgrounds |
| Yalpiz (mint) | `#DCFCE7` | Background of the mark |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | The house; the word on green |

White on `#15803D` passes WCAG AA for text. Do not recolor the mark: the shops have their own
colors, Zumda stays green.

## Rules

- Clear space around the mark: at least a quarter of its width.
- Smallest size: 16 px (the "Zumda asosida ishlaydi" line in a storefront).
- In a shop's storefront the shop leads; Zumda is the small line at the bottom.
- A shop's bot avatar is set by Zumda: the shop logo (or its name on its color) with the
  mark in a white circle at the bottom right.
